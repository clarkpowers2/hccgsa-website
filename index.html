THE PROBLEM WE NAMED

Every independent hotel in America
has Institutional Alzheimer's.

The moment a shift ends — everything your team learned about a guest that day disappears. The room that was too cold. The name they prefer. The problem that was never fully resolved.

Gone.

HCCGSA LLC uses the term Institutional Alzheimer's as an organizational metaphor — a deliberate framework to name the pattern of systemic operational memory loss that recurs in hotel environments at every shift change, staff departure, and system handoff. The descriptive term for this organizational pattern is Institutional Memory Loss.

Both terms are part of the HCCGSA LLC research framework authored by Nathaniel Clarke, ORCID: 0009-0005-5311-1358.

THE NAMED FRAMEWORK

The Memory Gap™

The Memory Gap™ is the distance between what a hotel knows about its guests and what it can actually act on — across shifts, staff turnover, and disconnected systems.

It is not a technology failure.
It is an institutional one.

26 guests leave silently for every one who complains. They do not call the front desk. They check out quietly. They book somewhere else. They never say why.

A 200-room independent hotel loses an estimated $4.3 million to $13.6 million every year from guests who had a problem, said nothing, and never came back.

The Memory Gap™ makes this loss visible, measurable, and closeable.

A CATEGORY WE INVENTED

Guest Intelligence OS™

Every hotel has a Property Management System for reservations. A CRM for marketing emails. A reputation tool for reviews.

None of them answer the question that matters most:

What does this guest need right now — based on everything that happened before?

Not after checkout. Not next week.
Right now. While they are still in the building.

We call this the Guest Intelligence OS™ — the operating system of guest memory. It sits above every system a hotel already uses. It does not replace anything. It remembers what everything else forgets.

Active Recovery

A guest signals a problem. AI responds in seconds. Staff acts before checkout. The review never gets written.

Persistent Memory

Every guest interaction is stored permanently. Every visit is informed by the last one. Your team never makes the same mistake twice.

Verified Intelligence

Guest data is confirmed and trusted. Your team acts on accurate information — not assumptions, not stale records. Facts.

THE PRIMARY METRIC

Guest Memory Score™

How do you know if your hotel is getting better at remembering its guests?

The Guest Memory Score™ is the executive metric that measures whether a property is recognizing guests, assigning ownership of issues, acting in time, and verifying recovery before checkout. It is the first measurable standard for institutional memory health in independent hotel operations.
